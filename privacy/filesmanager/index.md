# FilesManager — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

Files Manager is a local-first file explorer. It does not collect, track, or transmit your personal data, and it has no advertising or analytics.

The files you browse stay on your device or on the network locations you configure; nothing is sent to us. Connection details you enter (addresses, credentials) are stored only on your device to make those connections work.

Network features contact only the servers and devices you set up — nothing else. The optional on-device photo background removal downloads its model once through Google Play services and runs entirely on your device; your photos are never uploaded.

Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.

In short, your data is safe: we run no servers and no cloud infrastructure to host or upload anything, and we have no partners to share your data with. This app is built out of passion and a love of things done well, by an independent developer. The business model is simple: if you enjoy it, you're free to go premium for more features or leave a small donation. Nothing more! 🙂
