# WeatherScraper — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

This app collects nothing.

There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising. The cities you add, your settings and the last forecast for each city are stored on your device and sent nowhere.

It makes network requests only to addresses you gave it: the forecast pages you paste, and the forecast or geocoding service you configure. Those requests carry what any web request carries — your address on the network, and an identifier naming this app — and what those sites do with it is covered by their own policies, not this one.

A backup you export is written to the folder you choose, on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

---

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised on the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/Mambocoding/io/issues) of `Mambocoding/io`.
