# NotesManager — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

NotesManager is a local-first notes app. It does not collect, track or transmit your personal data, and it has no advertising or analytics.

Your notes stay on your device. If you pair the app with your own self-hosted hub, your notes sync only to that hub — nothing is sent anywhere else, and no third party ever sees them.

Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.

---

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised on the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/Mambocoding/io/issues) of `Mambocoding/io`.
