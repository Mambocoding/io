# TennisScraper — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

Your favorite players, settings and notification history are stored only on this device, in app-private storage — nothing is sent to the developer.

To show matches, rankings and news, the app contacts the service(s) configured in Settings → Data source directly from your device. No account is required and no personal data is collected by the app itself.

---

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised on the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/Mambocoding/io/issues) of `Mambocoding/io`.
