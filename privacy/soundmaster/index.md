# SoundMaster — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

SoundMaster collects no personal data and makes no network connection. A session's statistics are stored locally in this device's own storage, only when you choose to save them, and can be exported or deleted at any time.

---

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised on the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/Mambocoding/io/issues) of `Mambocoding/io`.
