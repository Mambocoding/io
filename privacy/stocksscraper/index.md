# StocksScraper — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

StocksScraper collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Your watchlist, settings and alert history are stored only on this device.

The only network requests this app makes are to fetch prices and news from the third-party sources listed in the Terms of use.

A backup, if you create one, is a plain file written to a folder you choose. It never leaves your device unless you move it yourself, and nobody but you can read it.

---

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised on the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/Mambocoding/io/issues) of `Mambocoding/io`.
