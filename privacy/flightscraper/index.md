# FlightScraper — Privacy policy

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

FlightScraper collects no personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

Your watched airports, watch rules, tracker links, movement log, alert history and settings are stored on this device. Nothing is sent to the developer. If your device's own backup is switched on, the system may include this app's data in it, under your account.

The app makes network requests only to addresses you gave it: the traffic feed you configure, the map tile address if you set one, and the airport pages you add to the timetable. Those requests carry what any web request carries — your address on the network — and what those services do with it is covered by their own policies. Opening a movement in a tracker you chose hands it to your browser.

A backup you export is written to the folder you choose, on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device.

---

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised on the [public issue tracker](https://github.com/Mambocoding/io/issues) of `Mambocoding/io`.
